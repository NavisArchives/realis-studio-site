Headquarters

Headquarters is the web app that runs Realis Studio day to day. It watches every client site and its admin, keeps small finishable missions moving, turns a list of prospects into outreach drafts written in the right language and the right brand voice, reads the replies that come back, and issues the invoices. It runs on Cloudflare with a single database and no framework: the same discipline the studio applies to client work, applied to itself. The screenshots on this page show sample data.
