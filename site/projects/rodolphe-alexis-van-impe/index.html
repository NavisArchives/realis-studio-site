Website

The work had to come first.

The brief was a site that disappears behind the photography: full-bleed images, a five-word navigation, project pages that load instantly on a phone. Type and spacing were tuned so the buildings are always the largest thing on screen, and the contact path stays one tap away.

The result

A portfolio site that puts the photography first: full-bleed projects, minimal chrome, fast on a phone.
