Questions

What does it cost?

Websites start at €1,200 and are priced on what they have to do. For software, a discovery sprint is €2,500 and projects start at €10,000, billed per phase. Care is priced in your proposal.

How long does it take?

Week 1 is the discovery sprint. The build follows in phases over weeks 2 to 9, for a first client portal.

How many feedback rounds?

Usually three, on the prototype, before anything is built.

Can we start small?

Yes. Start with the discovery sprint, then one phase at a time. You can stop between phases.

Who owns the software?

You do. The code, the accounts and the domain are in your name from the first day.

What happens after launch?

Care: updates, backups tested by restoring them, a response time in writing. It is priced in your proposal.
