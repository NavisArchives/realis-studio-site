Questions

What does it cost?

Websites start at €1,200 and are priced on what they have to do. For software, a discovery sprint is €2,500 and projects start at €10,000, billed per phase. Care is priced in your proposal.

Who owns the software?

You do. The code, the accounts and the domain are in your name from the first day.

What if Andy is away?

Cover is agreed in your care contract, and the documentation is written so another developer can step in.

Do you use AI?

Yes, where it saves hours on work a person can check. Nothing it produces counts until someone has approved it.

Can we start small?

Yes. Start with the discovery sprint, then one phase at a time. You can stop between phases.

Which languages?

Dutch, French and English, for the software and the website.

Where are you based?

Realis has its financial office in Tbilisi, for Eastern Europe, and a team in Brussels for Northern Europe. Calls between 13:00 and 18:00 Brussels time.

Tell us about your project.

We’ll tell you whether software is the answer, or whether a tool you can buy already does the job.
